# Target Sector Definition (MVP Beta)

## Objective
Define the first target business profile for beta rollout and map the minimum HACCP forms required for daily operations in ComplyFood.

## Selected target sector
- **Sector:** Independent restaurant / trattoria
- **Location:** Italy
- **Business size:** 1 location, 5–20 staff members
- **Operating model:** Lunch and dinner service with daily receiving, cold storage, prep, cooking, and cleaning routines

## Why this sector first
- Matches the current ComplyFood workflow coverage (temperature, receiving, cleaning, incidents, checklists, reminders, reports, documents)
- High paperwork repetition and clear compliance pain points
- Practical for onboarding 2–3 pilot businesses with similar daily routines

## Baseline operating profile
- 1 kitchen with cold storage and at least one freezer
- Daily supplier deliveries for perishable goods
- Opening and closing operational checks
- Cleaning and sanitation tasks across kitchen and service areas
- Designated admin/owner plus staff users

## HACCP form mapping for MVP beta

### 1) Temperature controls
- Cold room / fridge temperature log
- Freezer temperature log
- Hot-holding or cooked-food control check (where applicable)
- Corrective-action note for out-of-range values

### 2) Receiving checks
- Delivery intake log (supplier, lot/batch reference, arrival temperature when required, acceptance/rejection)
- Non-conformity flag for damaged packaging or temperature deviations

### 3) Cleaning and sanitation
- Opening cleaning checklist
- During-service hygiene checklist
- End-of-day cleaning and sanitation checklist
- Task completion confirmation with responsible operator

### 4) Incident and corrective actions
- Incident/anomaly record
- Mandatory corrective action description
- Follow-up verification status

### 5) Verification and audit support
- Daily completion review (admin)
- Weekly compliance summary export (PDF/CSV)
- Document attachments (supplier certificates, internal procedures, inspection evidence)

## Role setup for pilot businesses
- **Admin/Owner:** organization setup, schedules, presets, report export, document review
- **Staff:** daily form completion, checklist confirmation, override with mandatory reason
- **Auditor/Consultant (optional in pilot):** read-only verification of logs and reports

## Out-of-scope for initial sector wave
- Multi-site enterprise chains
- Manufacturing plants with specialized CCP workflows
- Full auditor portal and external system integrations

## Readiness criteria tied to this definition
- Presets and reminders configured for restaurant daily cadence
- End-to-end completion of all mapped forms by pilot users
- Exported reports and audit trail accepted as inspection-ready by pilot feedback
